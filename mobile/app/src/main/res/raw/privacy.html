CartShare Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Data Controller

CartShare is responsible for processing your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data We Collect

CartShare is designed as an offline-first application.

  • Local data: Shopping lists, products, and categories created by the user are stored locally on the device.
  • Firebase Analytics: Anonymous usage data such as app interactions and device information.

No personal identifiers such as name, email address, or contact lists are required to use the app.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Data processing is based on:

  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — Legitimate interest in improving app stability and performance.

4. Data Storage and Security

Local data remains on your device and is protected by Android’s security mechanisms.

Firebase services operate over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Data transfers outside the EEA are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

5. User Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Delete your data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app

6. Data Retention

Local data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app. Analytics data is retained according to Google’s retention policies.

7. Children’s Privacy

CartShare is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
